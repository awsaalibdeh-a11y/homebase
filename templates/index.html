What Homebase will never do

  • Hide itself or share anything in secret.
  • Read your other apps, your browsing or your private messages.
  • Switch on your microphone or camera by itself.
  • Lock your tablet or limit your screen time.

Location is kept for 7 days, then deleted. Leave the family any time and everything you shared is removed.

Set it up in three minutes

  1. On a parent's phone: install, choose Create a family, then Invite to get a code.
  2. On the tablet: install, choose Join with a code, read what is shared, and say yes.
  3. Allow location, notifications and the microphone, and turn off battery saving for Homebase so it can ring.

Installing from outside the Play Store: when Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
