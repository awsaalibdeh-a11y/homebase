What is shared, and what never is

What Homebase does

  • Shares your location while sharing is on. You can pause any time and your family sees that it is paused.
  • Counts minutes spent playing or watching, per app, if you turn screen time on.
  • Lets a parent lock a device (never more than 4 hours a day) or ground apps for a while. You can always ask to be unlocked or send an SOS.
  • Tells the family whenever a lock, grounding or new device happens, so nothing is a surprise.

What it never does

  • Hide itself, or do anything in secret. Every device has an icon and every action is announced.
  • Read your messages in other apps, your browsing, your files, or what is on your screen.
  • Switch on your microphone or camera by itself.
  • Count homework or school apps as screen time.

Location is kept for 7 days, screen time for a month, then both are deleted. You can delete your own data, or leave the family, any time.

Set it up in three minutes

  1. On a parent's phone or laptop: Create a family, then Invite to get a code for each person.
  2. On a child's phone: install the Android app, choose Join with a code, read what is shared, and say yes.
  3. On a Windows computer: open Menu, Screen time, Link a Windows computer, download the tracker, and type the 6-digit code. If two brothers share one laptop, it asks who is using it.
  4. Allow location, notifications and the microphone, and turn off battery saving for Homebase so it can ring.

Installing from outside the Play Store: when Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser. On Windows, if it says it protected your PC, press More info, then Run anyway.
