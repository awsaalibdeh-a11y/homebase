🏠 Update Homebase

A newer Homebase is ready, with screen time on the Family tab and everything in just two tabs. Pick one way below. Your family, places and settings stay exactly as they are.

Way 1: Use the web version never needs updating

Always the newest, and it updates itself. You only do this once.

  1. Open Chrome on your phone and go to homebase-family.onrender.com/app
  2. Sign in with your family code or your account.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right), then Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. Open the new Homebase icon. You can remove the old app.
Open the web version

Way 2: Install the new Android app

Does a bit more in the background (location, grounded apps). Updates after this are one tap.

  1. Tap Download the new app below.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open on the download (or open it from your notifications or the Files app).
  3. If Android asks, tap Settings, allow Install unknown apps for this browser, then go back.
  4. Tap Install or Update. It installs over the old one, so you stay signed in.
